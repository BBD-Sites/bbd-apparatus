# Plan

## When to use
Use this when the owner says what they need to do, asks what today looks like, wants a weekly review, asks what they are waiting on, says what they finished, or tells you their plans have changed.

## Steps
Dump. Take anything they say. Add each to-do as one line under its project page in 30-projects/: `- [ ] text | area: name | due: 2026-10-14`. Waiting on someone: add `| waiting: Name since 2026-10-01`. Repeats: add `| every: 30d`. If you meet a name you do not know, ask once, write the answer in the vault, and never ask again. Every effort is its own project. Ask the owner for no other step.

Today. Make one short list. Put on it what is due today or tomorrow or late, the week's Big 3, and anything waited on past the owner's limit (7 days unless RULES.md says another) with the name and the days. Order by tier (the area order in RULES.md), then by date, then Big 3 first within a tier. There are no priority labels. An undated item that is not a Big 3 stays off. When asked about one business or area, show only its items.

Week. At the weekly review, fill in last week from the pages: what was done, what was stuck, last Big 3. Ask only for the three things for next week, and write them to 30-projects/plan/week-<date>.md. Draw the choices from 30-projects/plan/horizons.md. Show the next dated milestone under each area. One review covers every business.

Horizons. horizons.md holds day, week, month, year and five years at once. Roll the near ones from the far ones. When something changes, such as a sale or a new aim, update every horizon in the same turn and ask nothing twice.

Done. Change `[ ]` to `[x]` and add `| done: date`. A repeat comes back on its day with the new last-done date. When asked what they got done, list the [x] items done in the period, grouped by area, each with its date. Never put a done item on Today.

## Never
- Never ask the owner to sort, label or file.
- The brief is off. It is set to never, and no push, text or email is wired. The owner opens the app to see the plan.
- This skill sends nothing to anyone. It writes to the repository and answers in the chat.
