# Intake

## When to use
Use this when a new owner opens their first session, or when they say they want to add a business, a project or a part of their life to the system. If RULES.md already exists, run only the area step for the one new thing and leave the rest alone.

## Steps
1. Ask who they are and what they run, in their words. One question at a time.
2. Ask how they want you to talk to them, and what they want remembered. Write it down as they say it.
3. Ask their faith, in plain words: "Is faith part of your life and work?" If they name one, write it down in their words and their tradition. Only then say that a free shelf of old public works can go in their library, and ask if they want it. If they say no, or say faith is not part of this, say fine, and keep advice neutral.
4. Offer each area, and say none is needed and any can be added later by just saying so: each business they run, family, faith, health, money. Offer money once, lightly. Say that bookkeeping is a separate thing and not part of this. If they pass, move on and do not ask again.
5. Ask the order of weight. The first is tier 1, the next is tier 2, and so on.
6. For each area ask: "Is there something you are working on there right now?" Each answer becomes its own project.
7. Write the files. RULES.md at the top has who they are, how to talk to them, what to remember, their faith and shelf answer, and the areas in order. Each area is a folder 40-areas/<name>/ with a README.md holding its tier. Each project is a page 30-projects/<name>.md. If one exists, do not make it twice.
8. If they said yes to the shelf, write 70-library/scripture/README.md with the day and their tradition. The shelf itself is placed later by the platform.
9. Read it all back as a short list and ask what to fix. Fix it, then read back only what changed.

## Never
- Never ask where recordings, transcripts or files are kept.
- Never make an area required. Never press on money.
- Never bring up scripture for a buyer who did not ask for it.
- Never ask a question you can decide yourself. Ask only what no default can answer.
- This skill sends nothing to anyone. It only writes files in the repository.
